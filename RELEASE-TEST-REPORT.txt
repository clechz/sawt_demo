BUGS FOUND AND FIXED

1. Looked up an explicitly requested dish but failed to save it before asking a
   follow-up. The planner now gets an explicit save requirement.
2. Repeated sugar questions and lost size/ice on a correction. Saudi half-sugar
   wording is recognized; known required choices survive unrelated edits.
3. A bare yes to a question lacked context. The exact pending choice is now
   stored with its line and revision. Yes accepts only one proposed choice;
   expiry, replacement, interruption/topic changes and stale revisions cannot
   turn it into order confirmation.
4. Asking about karak ice sounded unnatural. The assistant proposes no ice and
   waits for agreement; it does not silently modify the source menu or assume it.
5. Optional sauce removal was initially overprotected. Required-choice
   preservation no longer reverses explicit optional sauce removal.
6. Tamees dish names were confused with bread changes; generic fool could become
   Hijazi without clarification. Dish variants and modifier evidence are checked.
7. Unsupported delivery destinations produced a pickup follow-up. Delivery intent
   and the actual coverage error are now preserved.
8. Free-text confirmation invitations could bypass the exact readback request.
   Observed invitation wording now goes through checkout/readback gating.
9. Synthetic audio could start before the voice worker was ready and lose its
   opening words. The local lab disables Send until the ready event.
10. Native form navigation failed in this Chrome setup. The private login uses
    same-origin submission with explicit errors and a hash-bound script policy;
    Chrome login was then verified successfully.

LIMITS / WHAT THESE TESTS DO NOT PROVE

- This is ready for invited demo testing, not immediate restaurant production.
  There is no real POS, payment flow, kitchen dispatch or human call transfer.
- Synthetic speech is not a native Saudi listener's accent rating. Pronunciation,
  noisy callers, different microphones and regional phrasing need further human
  review. No claim of 100% accuracy or exhaustive coverage is made.
- Some turns remain slow: the 24-turn planner replay had a 1,540.5 ms median and
  4,596 ms maximum backend turn time. The last published voice smoke observed
  saved delegation state 8.39 seconds after synthetic input ended; this includes
  turn detection/model work/polling and is not time-to-first-audio. Exact readback
  was about 29–30 seconds for the two-item order. Latency remains an improvement
  area; these are small-sample measurements, not a production SLA.
- No sustained load/soak test, mobile device matrix or independently hosted
  uptime verification was performed. Calls are capped at 2 active, 20 starts/hour,
  and 10 minutes each in this demo.
- The app records calls locally for review with its existing retention process.
  The public gateway blocks archives. Read-only review of previous human calls
  used transcripts, tools and audit records; it was not an acoustic listening test.
- The first attempted synthetic browser call lost startup words and was discarded.
  The subsequent completed call and final ready-gated complaint test are the
  cited evidence. A test-only LiveKit cleanup warning was fixed by closing its
  audio resources; the final published smoke exited cleanly.
