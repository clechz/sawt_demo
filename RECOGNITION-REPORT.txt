SAWT RECOGNITION INVESTIGATION — 2026-10-12

Actual complaint and cause
In call ec22739db61847d9a813ba0c80de3d1d, the live captions rendered the
complaint as "ما قاعد أتكلم" (I am not speaking). The user confirmed that they
meant "أنت ما قاعد تتكلم" (the bot was not speaking). Re-transcribing the
approved final audio segment with GPT-Transcribe recovered the latter meaning.
The earlier whole-recording secondary transcription returned only a price
question and was incomplete; it was not reliable ground truth for the call.

The application had treated short, imperfect live captions as finalized commands.
SDK inspection found its is_final closes a caption segment after an audio gap;
it is not a provider-confirmed utterance boundary. A wrong pronoun could therefore
activate the opposite behavior. The SDK's placeholder confidence=1 was not an
accuracy signal. No evidence establishes a microphone/hardware fault: capture
reported no dropped chunks and only ~0.00036% clipped samples. Raw device audio
before browser processing was not available, so echo/noise causes are not ruled out.

Changes
- Retain a bounded 45-second PCM ring of the same audio supplied to GPT-Live.
- Join caption fragments and check the corresponding audio with GPT-Transcribe
  before transactional actions or pause/hang-up/recovery controls.
- Preserve 1.4 seconds of pre-roll and 450 ms of post-roll. An initial 350 ms
  pre-roll test cut off "كم" and misread a question as an item request. Testing
  the same clip with wider context retained the question; the final replay passed.
- Use Arabic/English language hints and a small restaurant vocabulary.
- Ask for clarification when verification fails, audio timing is invalid, or
  the two recognitions disagree on selected critical signals: price-question
  prefix, explicit negation, or quantity. This is a bounded heuristic.
- Discard results when newer speech changes the call epoch/fragment boundary.
- Distinguish inability to hear the bot from asking the bot to wait silently.
- Generic shawarma cannot silently choose chicken or beef. Unstated variants
  require clarification before adding/replacing an item.
- Each voice call owns its provider connection pool; one cleanup cannot close
  another call's HTTP pool. A captured session reference fixes a post-disconnect
  lifetime-task error. These code findings do not establish the cause of every
  earlier browser disconnect.

Latency/cost tradeoff
The six bounded ASR requests in the original-audio replay took 326–666 ms, median
447 ms. These are recognition request durations, not total reply latency or a
production p95. Verification adds a provider request and cost to non-social
utterances. GPT-Live still owns conversational timing; no second live VAD was added.

Limits
An earlier segment around 12–25 seconds remains unclear. No claim that every word
in the call has been recovered. Tests do not establish 100% recognition, accent
naturalness, every microphone/noise condition, broad load reliability or production
readiness. ASR and disagreement heuristics can both fail on new phrasing. Further
native-human microphone testing is needed, with explicit expected meanings.
